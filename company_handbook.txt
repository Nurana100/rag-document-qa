COMPANY HANDBOOK

Section 1: Vacation Policy
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid vacation per year. Vacation days accrue monthly and can be carried over into the next year, up to a maximum of 5 unused days. Employees must submit vacation requests at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal.

Section 2: Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require director-level sign-off and are reviewed every 6 months. All remote employees must be reachable during core hours, which are 10 AM to 3 PM in their local time zone.

Section 3: Expense Reimbursement
Employees can be reimbursed for business-related expenses including travel, client meals, and
software subscriptions required for their role, provided receipts are submitted within 30 days
of the purchase. Reimbursement requests submitted after 30 days require additional manager
approval and may be denied at the finance team's discretion.

Section 4: Equipment Policy
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and standard peripherals within their first week. Equipment requests beyond the standard package must be approved by the employee's manager and IT.

Section 5: Parental Leave
Full-time employees are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or fostering of a child. This leave can be taken continuously or split into two blocks within the first 12 months. Employees must notify HR at least 30 days before the expected leave start date when possible.
